Runner's Block — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026

Runner's Block ("the app") is made by Garrison Software, an individual developer. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is kept, and who can see it.

In short: your runs stay on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, no ads and no analytics.

What the app collects

DataWhenWhy
Precise location (GPS points, speed, time) Only while you are recording a run. The app uses a foreground service with a visible notification, and never runs in the background otherwise. It does not request background location permission. To measure your distance and pace and draw your route.
What's playing (song title, artist, album, track length, and which app is playing it) Only if you grant notification access. Songs are read from your music app's media controls and are saved only as part of a run you record. To work out your pace during each song.
Your settings When you change them. To remember your preferences.

The app does not read the content of your notifications, your contacts, your files, or anything else on your phone.

Where it is stored

Everything above is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. There is no server, and the app does not upload your runs or songs anywhere.

  • Android backup: if Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your own Google account, as with other apps. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
  • Exports: if you choose to export a run (as a JSON file), it goes only where you send it, using Android's share sheet.

Third-party services

The route map uses the Google Maps SDK for Android. To show map tiles, the SDK sends Google limited device and usage data: device model and OS version, IP address, crash diagnostics, a pseudonymous SDK identifier, and map interactions such as panning and zooming. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy; see Google's Maps SDK data disclosure. Your recorded route is drawn on the map on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Sharing and selling

Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. The developer has no access to it.

Deleting your data

Delete a run in the app to remove it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
